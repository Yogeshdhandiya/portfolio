# Reference style guide (snapgram ajti3pwis1ck, Ashmit Shahi, 51s)

Format: 720x1280 at 24fps. Loudness -14 LUFS. Talking head at a desk, moody teal/blue background with a warm practical lamp. Speech runs at about 3.7 words/s with no dead air, so jump cuts are tight.

## Structure
- Hook: 0 to 7s. Name lower-third, then a "99%" glow stat above the head.
- Three numbered sections ("Number one / two / three"). Each one opens with a full-screen BLUE CARD: a big ghosted "#01" plus a label ("Camera") over a hero image.
- Explanation beats cut to blue product cards (Amazon-style screenshots tilted on a blue grid, with a ₹ price counter).
- CTA ending: "Comment VIDEO" with the keyword as a glowing quoted word.

## Captions
- 1 to 3 words at a time, white, bold geometric sans (Sora-like), small (about 4.5% of frame height), centred at about 62% of the height, over the chest so the face stays clear.
- KEYWORD EMPHASIS (about 1 every 4 to 6s): the word gets 2 to 3x bigger with an electric-blue glow (#3fb6ff core, #0a6cff halo), sometimes with a small white lead-in line above or below ("of the / creators", "Cinematic / look").
- A second emphasis style uses a gold/cream gradient serif-ish fill ("personal brand").
- Some words are animated letter by letter ("Cinematic", "high quality").

## Graphics
- Blue grid card: #0b4fd6 to #0a3ea8 gradient with a faint 80px grid, hard cut in and out, a slight push-in.
- Section number: huge outlined/ghosted "#01" at the top, label below in white.
- Price counter: big pale-white "₹4,52,741" counting up.
- 01/02/03 blue tiles pop in at bottom-left when "three things" is said.
- Camera REC UI overlay (corner brackets, REC timer, record button) on "record from your iPhone".
- Name lower-third: blue hand-drawn arrow, white name and a title line.
- One full-screen orange/red B-roll accent card ("B-roll my own").

## Motion and sound
- Punch-ins: alternate between wide and about 115% tight framing on cuts, to hide jump cuts.
- Pops and whooshes on card entries, a light tick on caption keywords, low music bed.
