# Style: "Blue Glow Creator" (reference: Ashmit Shahi reel, snapgram ajti3pwis1ck, 51 s)

Frame-level breakdown at 8–12 fps of every section. This is the house style Yogesh asked to match.

## Global
- 9:16, 24 fps, -14 LUFS, music bed + dense SFX (spectrogram: continuous sub/bass bed, sustained synth notes around 1–1.5 kHz, a broadband transient every 1–2 s).
- Palette: deep blue (#0b4fd6 → #04236e), electric-blue glow (#3fb6ff core, #0a6cff halo), warm gold gradient for the second emphasis colour, white text.
- Pacing: tight jump cuts at sentence starts. Framing alternates wide and tight at every cut. Long shots get a slow push.
- Grade: moody, slight crush, vignette.

## Captions (most important element)
- Position: chest, about 60–63% of frame height, always below the chin and never over the face.
- **Built word by word.** Each word fades in with a short blur (about 4 frames) as it's spoken, and words accumulate into a stacked phrase.
- **Stacked hierarchy:** small lead-in line (about 34 px), then a BIG emphasis word (about 120–150 px), then a small tail line. Examples: "of the / CREATORS / on instagram.", "But my / VIDEOS / looks better than", "and build / A PREMIUM → PERSONAL / brand."
- Emphasis types (one per phrase, about every 3–6 s):
  1. **Blue glow**: electric-blue text with layered glow.
  2. **Gold gradient**: cream → gold → amber vertical gradient.
  3. **Big plain white**: heavy white word ("but").
  4. **Letter scramble**: letters of a glow word appear in random order ("C _ m _ c" → "Cinematic").
  5. **Chip**: rounded blue pill with a white border and a 4-point sparkle star on the top-right corner, popping in with overshoot ("motion graphic", "neat typefaces").
- Plain caption chunks: 1–3 words, white, semibold, centred.
- Exit: blur-fade out over about 4 frames (no hard pop off).

## Rotoscoping
- Hook stat "99%" sits **behind the head** (person matte over the text), types on character by character, glows, and stays about 3 s while captions continue below.

## Hook (0–3 s)
- Opens tight, then a smooth eased pull-back. Zoom-blur + RGB-split transition into the next shot.
- Name lower-third: hand-drawn blue arrow draws on (stroke animation), then name and title fade in.

## Section cards ("#01 Camera", "#02 Lighting", "#03 Editing")
- Hard cut in on the jump cut. Full-screen blue-duotone hero image (camera, softbox, editing timeline) with a slow push-in.
- Big "#0N" at the top: bold grotesk with a white→transparent vertical gradient.
- Label below ("Camera") blur-fades in about 0.3 s after the cut.
- Duration about 1.3–1.6 s, hard cut out.

## Info cards (products)
- Blue grid background, product screenshots on white cards, small grey labels.
- **Price counter** counts up fast with motion blur (₹65,358 → ₹6,75,728 in about 0.7 s).
- Caption sits on the card under the image.

## Overlays on the talking head
- Camera REC UI on "record from your iPhone": thin white viewfinder brackets, red REC timer box counting, record button + 1x pill sliding up from the bottom.
- 01/02/03 blue tiles with labels pop in on "three things".
- Full-screen colour-graded B-roll with a big white word ("B-roll") + small "my own".

## CTA
- "I'll send you" small + big glow "“VIDEO”" with outlined quote marks, then plain captions to the end.
